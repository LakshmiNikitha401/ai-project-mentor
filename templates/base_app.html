Platform Guide

1. Discover Projects

Use the search bar or explore domains. You can also click "Brainstorm My Idea" to build a custom project with the AI.

2. Start a Roadmap

When you find a project, click "Start Project". Select your timeline, and the AI will generate a step-by-step technical roadmap for you.

3. Ask the AI Mentor

Stuck on a bug or need dataset ideas? Go to the AI Mentor, select your project context, and ask any technical question.

4. Project Analyzer

Have a previous Minor project? Upload the report to the Analyzer, and the AI will suggest how to expand it into a Major project.

5. Academic Studio

Use this to generate your Abstract, Methodology, Viva Questions, and Final Report based on your active project.
